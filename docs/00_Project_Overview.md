# Sports Gurukul

# Project Overview

Version: 1.0

## Executive Summary

Sports Gurukul is an AI-first digital sports ecosystem connecting athletes, coaches, academies, parents, scouts, sponsors and tournament organizers on a unified platform.

## Vision
To become the world's most trusted AI-powered sports ecosystem.

## Mission
Empower every athlete with technology, coaching and career opportunities.

## Core Modules
- Authentication
- Athlete Management
- Coach Management
- Academy ERP
- Booking
- Payments
- Tournament
- AI Coach
- Marketplace
- Community
- Analytics
