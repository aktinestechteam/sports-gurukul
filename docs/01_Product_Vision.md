# Sports Gurukul

# Product Vision

Version: 1.0

## Vision Statement

To build the world's most trusted AI-powered sports ecosystem where every athlete can learn, train, compete and grow.

## Mission

Digitize the complete sports ecosystem by connecting athletes, coaches, academies, parents, scouts and sponsors.

## Value Proposition

- Athlete Marketplace
- Coach Marketplace
- Academy ERP
- AI Coach
- Video Analysis
- Tournament Platform
- Sponsorship Marketplace

## Business Model

- Coach Subscription
- Academy Subscription
- Booking Commission
- Marketplace Commission
- AI Premium Features
- Enterprise Licensing

## Long Term Goal

Become the operating system for sports.
